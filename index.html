Australia • Greenhouse Gas Emissions • 2017–2022

Tracing Australia’s Greenhouse Gas Story

Australia’s greenhouse story is one of ambition and transformation.
From bustling cities to vast forests, every region plays a part.
Between 2017 and 2022, emissions rose, fell, and reset priorities.
COVID lockdowns caused a dramatic dip — a pause in impact.
Recovery brought new challenges and a sharper focus on net zero.

Queensland and New South Wales dominate national emissions overall,
while Tasmania stands out — quietly absorbing more than it emits.
Scroll to uncover how each state’s story unfolds across years,
and see who’s truly steering Australia’s climate future forward.
